You can view the next reset time in Claude’s usage settings.

That gives the plan a more specific meaning:

> Claude Max 5x offers substantially more usage headroom than Pro, but it is still a capacity-based subscription with session and weekly limits.

- Work through long research documents.

Anthropic specifically positions Max for people who collaborate with Claude frequently and need higher usage capacity. Max subscribers also receive priority access to newer models and features, and the plan includes access to Claude Code for terminal-based coding workflows.

1. Do you regularly receive usage-limit warnings?

5. Would switching to an API-based setup create more complexity than it saves?

If most answers are no, Pro may be sufficient. If the answer to the first three questions is consistently yes, Max 5x becomes easier to justify.

## How BeWild’s Claude Max subscription process works

BeWild’s published Claude tutorial describes a four-step process:

1. Choose the Claude product and subscription duration.
2. Log in to your own Claude account and export the required browser cookie.
3. Paste the cookie data into the BeWild subscription page for verification.
4. Complete payment and wait for the subscription upgrade.

BeWild says the activation process usually takes a few minutes after payment, although the published help page gives a broader estimated activation window of approximately **5 to 30 minutes**.

The process is different from a normal direct credit-card subscription. BeWild’s documentation says Claude activation requires a Cookie-Editor browser extension and a JSON export from the logged-in Claude page. The exported data is then submitted for account verification.

This is the part that deserves the most attention. A browser cookie can act as an active login credential. Anyone who obtains a valid session cookie may be able to access the associated account, depending on how the service and session are configured.

If you use this type of activation process:

- Only use your own Claude account.
- Never send a cookie file to an individual through an unrelated messaging app.
- Do not reuse the exported JSON for another purpose.
- Avoid copying cookies from Google, ChatGPT, or another website.
- Remove or restrict the browser extension after the process if you no longer need it.
- Change your account password or revoke active sessions if you suspect the credential was exposed.
- Confirm that the email address shown in Claude matches the account you intended to upgrade.

BeWild’s own guide says the cookie should be copied from the logged-in Claude page, should be kept private, and should not be shared with other people.
